Hugo Muñoz Ureña

PhD (Law) · Blockchain Governance Engineer · Associate Investigator, Chaire DDSA, Université Laval

Between a contract and the code that performs it lies an intermediate text that no one signs. I work on whether that text is faithful to the contract, and on who should be able to show it.

Current field: tokenized securities under South Korea's electronic registration framework. Method: the first leap, from binding text to executable rules, verified by an independent third party and recorded before the code acts.

Public comments to regulators

Financial Services Commission (Korea) — Public Notice No. 2026-670, amendments to the Enforcement Decree of the Electronic Registration Act and to the Electronic Registration Business Regulation

Personal-capacity opinion, 10 November 2026 (Seoul) · in favor of the draft, with one supplementary opinion: that draft Decree Art. 17-3 (5) name the procedure for confirming the correspondence between the business rules applied by a ledger's smart contracts and the terms of issue, and that a dated correspondence record, by a person with no part in drafting those rules or operating the ledger, be among the evidence documents · summary and proposed wording in Korean, body in English · CROSSWALK No. 1 as attachment

Read together, the texts verify the code against the business rules, and the ledger against the law. What no document records is whether the business rules correspond to the issuance documents.

U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission — Transfer Agent Rules (File No. S7-2026-30)

Personal-capacity comment letter, 30 October 2026 · published in the Commission's comment file, with CROSSWALK No. 1 as enclosure · proposes that Rule 17ad-12 name the risk that business rules applied by automated means are not faithful to the binding instrument they implement, and that a competent person with no part in that translation verify them

The verification does not create that responsibility. It creates the evidence with which to discharge it.

Monetary Authority of Singapore — Consultation on Proposed Amendments to the Payment Services Act 2019 for Stablecoins Regulation (Consultation Paper P015-2026)

Personal-capacity response, 8 September 2026 · proposes the distinction between verified capability and declared capability, and the capability attestation as a document

Money is verified; code is declared.
